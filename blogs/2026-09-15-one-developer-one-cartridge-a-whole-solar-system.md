Fall from Space: An open world action RPG built for Chromatic, with original box art painted by Mitsuhiro Arita You wake up on a planet you do not recognize, with no memory of how you got there. That is everything Fall from Space hands you at the start. Where you go next, who you help, and who you leave behind is entirely up to you.
