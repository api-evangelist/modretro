PREPARE FOR A HISTORIC TETRIS MOMENT On Friday, June 7th at the Pasadena Convention Center, ModRetro in collaboration with the Tetris World Championship, will host a special, live streamed Tetris tournament. Witness top professional Tetris players go head-to-head with a celebrity competitor in the inaugural ModRetro Tetris Championship. Eight contestants will battle it out on the Chromatic system with our new version of Tetris, vying for the title of ModRetro Tetris Champion.
