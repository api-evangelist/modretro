A unique 1993 throwback that was ported to the SNES as Radical Rex, Baby T-Rex is a captivating platform game where the Jurassic meets the Cenozoic era! You are Rex, a rockin’ skateboarding dinosaur, on a mission to save Rexette, your girlfriend, from the evil magician Sethron. Sethron kidnapped and hid her in his spooky castle to conduct his sinister experiments.
