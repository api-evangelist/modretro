Rayman 2: The Great Escape was, by any measure, a triumph. Released in 1999 across N64, Dreamcast, PC, and PlayStation, it set a new bar for 3D platformers and cemented Rayman as one of the defining characters of that console generation. Ubisoft knew they had something, and wanted to get it into more players' hands…literally.
