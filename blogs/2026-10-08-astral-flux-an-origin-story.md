Having crash landed on a desolate alien planet, you must salvage enough scrap to repair your ship and make your way home. Along the way you will encounter strange creatures, ancient alien machines, and maybe even a new friend! Astral Flux: Origins is a prequel to Astral Flux , which was released on PC and Nintendo Switch™ in 2022.
