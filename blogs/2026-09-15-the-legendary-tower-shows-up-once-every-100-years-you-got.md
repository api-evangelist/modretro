In a sun drenched land somewhere far from here, pineapples live a quiet life with one shared dream: reaching a place called Pineapple Paradise. The only way in is the Legendary Tower, and the tower only appears once every 100 years. Its challenges are severe enough that nobody has ever dared to walk in.
